# Master Bibliography

## Research Phase References

1. Davis, S., & Mermelstein, P. (1980). Comparison of parametric representations for monosyllabic word recognition in continuously spoken sentences. *IEEE Transactions on Acoustics, Speech, and Signal Processing*, 28(4), 357-366.

2. Young, S., Evermann, G., Gales, M., Hain, T., Kershaw, D., Liu, X., ... & Woodland, P. (2002). *The HTK book (for HTK version 3.4)*. Cambridge University Engineering Department.

3. Hinton, G., Deng, L., Yu, D., Dahl, G. E., Mohamed, A. R., Jaitly, N., ... & Kingsbury, B. (2012). Deep neural networks for acoustic modeling in speech recognition: The shared views of four research groups. *IEEE Signal Processing Magazine*, 29(6), 82-97.

4. Oord, A. V. D., Dieleman, S., Zen, H., Simonyan, K., Vinyals, O., Graves, A., ... & Kavukcuoglu, K. (2016). Wavenet: A generative model for raw audio. *arXiv preprint arXiv:1609.03499*.

5. Zhang, Y., Pezeshki, M., Brakel, P., Zhang, C., Bengio, Y., & Pal, C. (2017). Towards end-to-end speech recognition with deep convolutional neural networks. *arXiv preprint arXiv:1701.02720*.
